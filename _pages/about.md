I am an **Adjunct Professor at the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA)**, Brazil, affiliated with the Institute of Computing and the Graduate Program in Computer Science (PGCOMP). I also conduct research at the **Centre for Data and Knowledge Integration for Health (CIDACS/Fiocruz)**.

My research focuses on **large-scale data integration, entity resolution, and record linkage**, with particular interests in machine learning, geocoding, and algorithmic fairness. I develop methods and computational tools to improve the quality and reliability of linked data, supporting research in public health and social inequalities.

I hold a PhD and an MSc in Computer Science from UFBA.
